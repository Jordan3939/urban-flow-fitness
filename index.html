About The Gym

Training for how athletes actually move

Urban Flow Fitness started as a mobility clinic for a local running club and grew into a full cross-training gym. We work with runners, climbers, lifters, and weekend athletes who need more than just another workout.

Every class is coached by trainers who understand sport-specific demands — because generic stretching doesn't fix what marathon training or heavy squats do to your body.

  • Coaches with sport-specific training backgrounds
  • Mobility work built around real training loads
  • Recovery sessions programmed around your race or lift calendar

Why Athletes Train Here

The edge your sport alone won't give you

Cross-training with us closes the gaps that running, lifting, or riding alone leave open.

Better Running Economy

Improved hip and ankle mobility translate directly into a more efficient stride.

Stronger Lifts

Core stability and shoulder mobility work support heavier, safer lifting.

Faster Recovery

Targeted mobility and breathwork reduce soreness and speed recovery between sessions.

Injury Prevention

Balanced training corrects the asymmetries that repetitive sport movement creates.

Mental Toughness

Breath control under fatigue builds composure you can call on mid-race or mid-lift.

More Explosive Power

Improved mobility unlocks force production your body couldn't access before.
